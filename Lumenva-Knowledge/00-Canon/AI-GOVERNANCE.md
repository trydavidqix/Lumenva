# AI GOVERNANCE

## 1. Model Selection
- Models are chosen based on fitness for task:
  - Claude for reasoning, orchestration, and planning.
  - Gemini for GCP infra and deep Google ecosystem integration.
  - Codex for routine software generation.

## 2. Hallucination Mitigation
- AI outputs are assumptions until proven by evidence (TDD, typechecks, integration tests).
- Agents must cite sources from the Knowledge Core when making claims.

## 3. Alignment
- AI actions must align with the Prime Directive and the Constitution.
- Any action that degrades system integrity is strictly forbidden.
