# PRIVACY

## 1. Data Ownership
- The tenant owns their data. We are mere custodians.
- Data is never used to train global models without explicit consent.

## 2. Data Minimization
- Collect only what is necessary.
- Retain only as long as required.
- Purge relentlessly.

## 3. Compliance
- Strict adherence to LGPD, GDPR, and relevant privacy frameworks.
- Privacy by design: default settings must be the most privacy-preserving.
